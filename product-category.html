With a foundation laid in 2004, RITA Food & Drink Co., Ltd is one of Vietnam's most experienced juice manufacturers. With a factory scale of 42,000 m2 and more than 500 employees, we supply over 100,000 tons of our exceptional products to meet the market's demands. Throughout our journey, we have successfully established a fruit juice brand that garnered widespread acclaim and appreciation worldwide.

If you're searching for "fruit juice near me," look no further! Rita company offers many flavorful fruit juices that satisfy your cravings. We utilize the freshest fruits and employ meticulous processes to make fruit juice bursting with flavor and nutrients. Our modern manufacturer blends ingredients due to a particular traditional recipe. We ensure proper packaging to create convenient and delicious products that provide the benefits of tropical juice.

Our unwavering dedication to ensuring the highest quality of our products is fundamental to our success. Therefore, we meticulously adhere to international standards and certifications, such as HACCP, Halal, ISO, GMP, and more, guaranteeing that all our products meet rigorous quality benchmarks.

Rita's delectable product range encompasses a variety of delightful options, including orange juice, mango juice, tamarind juice, soursop juice, pineapple juice, peach juice, mangosteen juice, dragon fruit juice, tart cherry juice, apple juice, mixed fruit juice from tropical fruit, and more.

Besides the Vietnamese market, Rita company specializes in fruit juice export, delivering the highest quality beverages to over 200 countries and regions worldwide. In addition to our renowned Rita brand, we offer OEM products that provide flexibility in customizing ingredients, flavors, and packaging to meet our customers' unique demands.
