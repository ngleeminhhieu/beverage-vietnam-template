OUR KEY MILESTONES

2004

OPEN RITA

Established in VietNam, to make and sell canned juice.

2005

SUPERMARKETS

RITA juice entered most of the main supermarkets in VietNam.

2006

MORE PRODUCTS

The launch of Pet and Glass bottle production line. More products: Coconut water, Aloe vera juice, Energy drink and Coffee drink.

2008

EXPAND EXPORTS

01.2008: Started the oversea market. 05.2008: The first 2 oversea shipment, one was to UK, the other was to Nepal.

2010

ROUND THE WORLD

Exports exceeded sales in the domestic market. Products are popular all around the world.

2013

PP BOTTLE

PP bottle production line launched.

2015

INTERNATIONAL PRIZE

Won the innovative Prize at Shanghai SIAL.

2016

NEW FACTORY

Bigger and more modern factory launched. Capacity enlarged 5 times.

2018

ASEPTIC PRODUCTS

200ml Aseptic pack line launched, with a 1L aseptic line on the agenda.

2019

PP CUP PRODUCTS

PP Cup line finished. Factory expanded by more than 50,000 square meters.

2022

PET PRODUCTION LINE

New automatic PET production line launched.

PRODUCTION PROCESS

01

Raw Material Inspection

Built on trust and precision, our raw material inspection process ensures only the finest ingredients become part of every RITA Beverage.

02

Material Transport to Washing

Every ingredient is carefully transferred to the washing line through a controlled process, ensuring cleanliness, freshness, and uncompromised quality.

03

Washing Raw Material

Every ingredient is meticulously washed under strict quality controls, ensuring maximum cleanliness while maintaining its natural characteristics.

04

Changing Area

Designed to support world-class hygiene standards, the changing area ensures every team member is fully prepared before entering the controlled production environment.

05

Preliminary Processing Room

Advanced preparation processes and strict quality controls ensure every ingredient is production-ready while maintaining its freshness, safety, and integrity.

06

Filter Conveyor

An integrated filter conveyor system continuously transfers the beverage while maintaining product purity, operational efficiency, and strict hygiene standards.

07

Incubation Area

Products are carefully incubated under controlled temperature and humidity conditions to ensure optimal quality, consistency, and product stability.
